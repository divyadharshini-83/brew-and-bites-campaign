# Brew & Bites Campaign

A comprehensive, data-backed 7-step marketing campaign and portfolio case-study for a modern study café in **Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Tamil Nadu**, built with Google Antigravity.

---

## Important: Data Transparency & Sources

> [!NOTE]
> This is a practice project for a fictional café concept ("Brew & Bites") set in a real Trichy neighbourhood (Thillai Nagar).
> - **The competitors, neighbourhood, and colleges are real.**
> - **The brand name, persona, and street plot number are invented.**
> - For a complete attribution breakdown with Source URLs, access dates, and Status labels (`Verified`, `Estimate`, `Assumption`), please read:
> 👉 **[Data, Sources & Attribution Guide](DATA-AND-SOURCES-GUIDE.md)**

## Workflow Roadmap & Deliverables

1. **Step 0 & 1: Audience Research** ([`01-audience-research/`](01-audience-research/))
   - [`audience-profile.md`](01-audience-research/audience-profile.md): Trichy 18–30 student & young professional segmentation.
   - [`persona-priya.md`](01-audience-research/persona-priya.md): Priya Ramanathan (21, engineering undergrad - Status: `Assumption`).
   - [`research-log.csv`](01-audience-research/research-log.csv): Grounded observations from local reviews.

2. **Step 2: Competitor Analysis** ([`02-competitor-analysis/`](02-competitor-analysis/))
   - [`competitor-matrix.csv`](02-competitor-analysis/competitor-matrix.csv): Audit of 5 real Trichy cafés with Source URLs and access dates.
   - [`gap-analysis.md`](02-competitor-analysis/gap-analysis.md): 2x2 positioning matrix and 4 unserved market gaps.

3. **Step 3: Funnel & Channel Strategy** ([`03-funnel-channel-strategy/`](03-funnel-channel-strategy/))
   - [`funnel-strategy.md`](03-funnel-channel-strategy/funnel-strategy.md): Awareness $\rightarrow$ Consideration $\rightarrow$ Visit $\rightarrow$ Loyalty with Mermaid diagram.

4. **Step 4: Content & Copywriting** ([`04-content-copywriting/`](04-content-copywriting/))
   - [`content-bank.md`](04-content-copywriting/content-bank.md): 16 content concepts across 4 pillars with 3x caption styles.
   - [`copywriting-reflections.md`](04-content-copywriting/copywriting-reflections.md): 5 before/after AI vs. human editorial case studies.

5. **Step 5: SEO & Keyword Research** ([`05-seo-keywords/`](05-seo-keywords/))
   - [`keyword-strategy.csv`](05-seo-keywords/keyword-strategy.csv): 17 intent-mapped local and transactional search terms.
   - [`gbp-optimization-guide.md`](05-seo-keywords/gbp-optimization-guide.md): Google Business Profile 3-pack ranking playbook.

6. **Step 6: Social Calendar & KPIs** ([`06-social-calendar-kpis/`](06-social-calendar-kpis/))
   - [`7-day-calendar.md`](06-social-calendar-kpis/7-day-calendar.md): Daily launch schedule with POS tracking tags.
   - [`kpi-projections.csv`](06-social-calendar-kpis/kpi-projections.csv): Benchmark-modeled metrics dashboard with explicit formulas.
   - [`kpi-tracking-framework.md`](06-social-calendar-kpis/kpi-tracking-framework.md): Attribution modeling & electrical circuit analogy.

7. **Step 7: Portfolio Site & Career Presentation** ([`07-portfolio-site/`](07-portfolio-site/))
   - Responsive case-study site ([`index.html`](07-portfolio-site/index.html), [`style.css`](07-portfolio-site/style.css), [`app.js`](07-portfolio-site/app.js)).
   - [`proposal-summary.md`](07-portfolio-site/proposal-summary.md): 1-page freelance proposal for local café owners.
   - [`linkedin-post.md`](07-portfolio-site/linkedin-post.md): Case study launch post for LinkedIn.
   - [`resume-bullets.md`](07-portfolio-site/resume-bullets.md): Tailored resume bullets & interview talking points.
