## 2. Interview Talking Points (The "Why Should We Hire You?" Story)

### Q: "Tell me about a marketing project you built from scratch."
> *"I built the Brew & Bites Campaign, an end-to-end growth strategy for a study café in Trichy. Instead of treating marketing as vague creative guesswork, I approached it with an engineering mindset. I mined real reviews from local colleges like NIT Trichy and Bishop Heber, uncovered that students were leaving existing cafés due to a lack of power sockets and expensive menus, and built a 4-stage funnel around a ₹149 Study Fuel Combo and guaranteed booth power outlets. Every stage was tied to measurable attribution—from Instagram Reels reach down to POS coupon codes and WhatsApp loyalty stamps."*

### Q: "How do you leverage AI in your marketing workflow?"
> *"I use AI as a high-speed scaffolding assistant, not a strategic decision-maker. In this campaign, I used Antigravity to structure folders, format tables, and draft initial caption variants. However, I never let AI invent research data or fake numbers. I personally edited every caption to remove generic corporate clichés like 'haven of tranquility' and replaced them with hyper-local student realities like 'Trichy veyyil' and 'splitting bills on UPI'. The value of a modern marketer isn't generating AI text—it's having the critical judgment to know what real humans respond to."*

### Q: "How does your engineering background help in marketing?"
> *"In electrical engineering, you learn to analyze systems as closed circuits: if current drops, you don't just dump more voltage into the circuit; you find which resistor is heating up. In marketing, if conversions drop, throwing more money at ads is wasteful. I analyze where the friction is—whether it's unclear pricing on Google Maps, high app-download friction in loyalty, or weak CTAs in captions. That analytical rigor prevents wasted ad spend."*
