Step 0 & 1

The Challenge: Trichy's Café Market Gap

Why existing cafés in Trichy fail to retain student and remote worker demographics.

The Three Local Pain Points

  • The "Socket Shortage" Dilemma: Most aesthetic cafés in Thillai Nagar offer only 1–2 corner outlets, forcing students to compete for seats or leave when laptop batteries drain.
  • Extreme Weather Friction: Trichy temperatures regularly exceed 39°C. Students need air conditioning and cold drinks between 12 PM and 4 PM, but cafés run generic, non-seasonal menus.
  • Market Bifurcation: Spaces are either ₹700+ celebration destinations (too formal for regular visits) or dated chains charging premium prices with limited student connection.

The Brew & Bites Solution

Positioned as a proposed study café concept in Thillai Nagar where specialty cold brews meet student-friendly pricing, desk power sockets, and a comfortable atmosphere.

“I treat the marketing funnel as a connected system: top-of-funnel reach attracts attention, friction points (unclear pricing, lack of sockets, slow Wi-Fi) cause drop-offs, and in-store orders represent the final output. Reflections

What I Learned Working on This Project

Key takeaways from researching, drafting, and structuring this practice campaign.

1. Grounding Concepts in Local Realities

I learned that checking local review themes and weather patterns gave the campaign a specific purpose—addressing power socket availability and hot afternoon hours in Trichy rather than relying on generic café tropes.

2. Editing AI Drafts for Clarity

AI tools quickly drafted ideas, but I needed to review and rewrite the copy. I replaced generic promotional phrases like "haven of serenity" with plain details and clear pricing like "₹149 combo".

3. Structuring Measurement Upfront

Setting up UTM parameters and POS tracking codes before drafting content helped me link each marketing step to a specific action, keeping the plan organized and measurable.
