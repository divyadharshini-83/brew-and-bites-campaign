### Full Post Copy:

What happens when an Electrical Engineering student designs a full-funnel marketing campaign from scratch? 

You don't treat marketing as creative guessing. You treat the funnel like a closed-loop electrical circuit. ⚡☕

Over the past week, I engineered an end-to-end growth campaign for **Brew & Bites**, a modern study café targeting college students and young professionals in **Tiruchirappalli (Trichy)**.

Instead of letting AI hallucinate fake reviews and imaginary numbers, I mined real student reviews across local campuses (NIT Trichy, Bishop Heber, St. Joseph's) to find actual unserved friction points.

Here is what grounded research revealed:
1. **The "Socket Shortage":** Aesthetic cafés in Trichy offer great decor, but only 2 corner plug points. Students leave the moment laptop batteries drain.
2. **The 40°C Trichy Sun:** Extreme daytime heat creates massive demand for chilled AC and cold coffee between 12 PM and 4 PM—an afternoon lull most cafés fail to monetize.
3. **App Fatigue:** Students hate downloading 50 MB loyalty apps for a single store.

Here is how I structured the 7-step growth system:
🔹 **Audience Research:** Profiled "Priya" (21, engineering student balancing capstone projects, placement prep, and a ₹3,500 monthly budget).
🔹 **Competitor Audit:** Built a 5-café matrix (Parashy, CakeBee, CCD, etc.) spotting a massive gap for unpretentious, study-friendly spaces.
🔹 **Full-Funnel Strategy:** Mapped Awareness (Reels) $\rightarrow$ Consideration (Google Maps 3-Pack) $\rightarrow$ Visit (₹149 Study Fuel Combo) $\rightarrow$ Loyalty (WhatsApp 5-Stamp Card).
🔹 **Copywriting Bank & AI Diffs:** Wrote 16 content concepts across 3 tone variations. More importantly, documented "What I Changed and Why"—stripping AI fluff ("haven of serenity") and replacing it with transparent, hyper-local pricing ("₹199 / 4 = ₹50/head").
🔹 **Local SEO:** Engineered Google Business Profile attributes and keyword-rich review response scripts.
🔹 **Attribution Circuit:** Modeled 10 metrics tied to POS coupon codes (`STUDENT149`) and UTM schemas.

To bring everything together, I built a responsive, interactive case study website from scratch showcasing the full pipeline, competitor tables, and before-and-after copy comparisons.
