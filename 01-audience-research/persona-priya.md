## Psychographic & Behavioral Snapshot

* **The Striving Achiever:** Priya balances final-year engineering projects, campus placement interviews, and online certification courses. Her hostel room is noisy and lacks consistent air-conditioning during scorching Trichy afternoons.
* **Socially Connected:** She cherishes weekend coffee catch-ups with her core circle of 3 friends. Her group loves posting candid café aesthetics, book stacks, and foodie stories on Instagram.
* **Value-Sensitive Pragmatist:** She has a refined taste for good cold coffee and aesthetic spaces, but calculates cost-per-benefit meticulously. She will gladly spend ₹150 on a great experience, but balks at a ₹400 bill with hidden service charges.

## Core Frustrations & Pain Points

1. **The "Socket Hunt":** Entering a cafe only to find the two available wall plugs already taken by someone else's laptop.
2. **The Passive-Aggressive Stare:** Feeling unwelcome or pressured to order a second ₹250 drink after 45 minutes of laptop work.
3. **Weak Trichy Summer Countermeasures:** Walk-in cafes where the AC unit is underpowered, turning an afternoon study session into an uncomfortable sweatbox.
4. **Disappointing Drinks:** Commercial instant-powder cold coffees priced like specialty third-wave brews.
