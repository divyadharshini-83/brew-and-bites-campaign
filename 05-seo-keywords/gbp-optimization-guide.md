## 2. Essential Attributes Checklist

Google’s search algorithm ranks local businesses higher when attributes match conversational queries. Recommended attributes to configure in Google Business Profile:

- [x] **Accessibility:** Wheelchair-accessible entrance, Wheelchair-accessible seating.
- [x] **Amenities:** Free high-speed Wi-Fi (100 Mbps), Power outlets at every table, Restroom, Air conditioning (strong cooling).
- [x] **Atmosphere:** Casual, Cozy, Trendy, Quiet work-friendly hours (10 AM – 5 PM).
- [x] **Crowd:** College students, Groups, Family-friendly, LGBTQ+ friendly.
- [x] **Offerings:** Specialty craft coffee, Iced cold brews, Vegetarian options, Vegan milk alternatives, Late afternoon happy hours.
- [x] **Payments:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm (UPI QR codes), Credit/Debit cards, Cash.
- [x] **Service Options:** Dine-in, Takeaway, No-contact delivery, Kerbside pickup.
